Why we do this

Every item on our racks and shelves was donated by someone in this community. When you shop with us, you're not just finding a good deal — you're helping fund the outreach and mission work that this store exists to support. Volunteers sort, clean, and price everything by hand, and a portion of our shelves is set aside for pieces worth a special look — the kind of finds regulars come back for.
